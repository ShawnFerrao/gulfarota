Terms

Cancellation and refund policy.

→

Everyone attending the conference must register and pay the registration fees.

→

This rule does not apply to invited speakers, as the registration fee is waived for them.

→

We ensure that delegates wishing to cancel and refund their registration fees are well aware of the process.

→

Cancellations received 30 days prior to the event will receive a refund of the registration fees, less an administration charge of 30% of the conference fees.

→

Registration cancellations must be notified in writing by email to info@gulfaorta.com.

→

Cancellations made after this window, as well as 'no shows', are not eligible for a refund.

→

Refunds will be processed one month after the event completion date.
