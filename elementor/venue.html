Getting there

Find your way to the towers.

Guests will find themselves minutes away from iconic landmarks including the Museum of the Future, Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), and Dubai Mall, while being just a 15–20 minute drive from Dubai International Airport.

🚖

By Taxi / Ride-Hailing (Uber, Careem)

Simply input "Jumeirah Emirates Towers" as your destination. The hotel is a well-known Dubai landmark — drivers will recognise it instantly.

🚇

By Metro

Take the Red Line and stop at Emirates Towers Metro Station. The hotel is a 5-minute walk from the station via the air-conditioned walkway.

🚘

By Car (Self-Drive)

Set your GPS to: Jumeirah Emirates Towers, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai. Valet and paid parking are available at the hotel.

✈️

From Dubai International Airport (DXB)

By Taxi: Approx. 15–20 minutes.
By Metro: Take the Red Line from Terminal 1 or 3 to Emirates Towers Station (approx. 25 minutes).
