Our Mission

Raising the standard of aortic care.

"Aorta: An Organ of Change — Inspiring Solutions for Global Aortic Disease Management."

The Gulf Aorta Summit is an international experts' hub, bringing together specialists from around the world to advance knowledge in aortic disease and its appropriate surgical and interventional management.

GAS is a hybrid congress, crafting intelligent experiences under this theme, following the remarkable success of previous Gulf Aorta conferences.

The congress shares experience with well-esteemed, renowned international experts — encouraging discussion, building consensus and shaping the future of safe aortic management.

GAS creates educational initiatives and offers a scientific platform for an international gathering, encouraging clinicians who aim to enhance their skills, engage with experts, or showcase pioneering ideas.

The congress dives into cutting-edge research and explores groundbreaking advancements in aortic disease treatment, driving valuable professional growth.

Its genuine mission is to raise awareness across cardiovascular and radiology specialties, sharing clinical and therapeutic research and contributing to the global conversation on safe, novel treatments for complex aortic diseases and complications.
