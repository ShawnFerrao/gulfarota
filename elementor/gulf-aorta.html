The Gulf Aorta Story

The Educational Foundation of GAS

Tracing the roots of Gulf Aorta

Gulf Aorta embodies the educational legacy of the Gulf Aorta Summit (GAS) — a groundbreaking initiative built on a visionary mission to create a global hub for experts. This collaborative platform advances aortic care and education across the Gulf region and worldwide, built on strong partnerships with leading international institutions.

A decade in the making

From one workshop to a continental platform.

2014

Step Zero

The Gulf Aorta journey began with what its founders call Step Zero: a foundational push to launch focused workshops and educational training in aortic medicine, hosted at Al Qassimi Hospital and Cardiovascular Centre in collaboration with leading institutions across the UAE and GCC.

Driven by growing regional demand for advanced expertise and standardised practice, Step Zero — developed with prominent regional and international experts — set the stage for the educational framework that followed.

2014–2019

The Gulf Aorta Initiative

Twenty-eight Aorta–Peripheral Vascular workshops and masterclasses transformed regional vascular care, led by faculty including Prof. Bernhard Reimers, Prof. Gianpaolo Carrafiello, Prof. Dierk Scheinert, Dr. Ayman Alsibaie, Dr. Arne G. Schwindt and Dr. Ayman Salih, under the guidance of Prof. Arif Alnooryani, Head of Interventional Cardiology and CEO of Al Qassimi Hospital.

Founders Prof. Adel Aswad, Dr. Zaki Khalifa, Dr. Hussein Ahmed and Dr. Wael Alabbasi laid the groundwork for the Focused Aorta-Vascular Interventional Framework Group — later joined by vascular interventionists from across the GCC, whose expertise grew the initiative into a global mission spanning Gulf cardiovascular hospitals, specialised centres and societies: the Gulf Aorta Initiative.

In parallel, an academic fellowship at the University of Cologne, Germany (2015–2017) — led by Prof. Jan Brunkwall, Prof. Payman Majd and Prof. Mohamed Sharkawy — helped build a robust algorithm for managing aortic disease, setting a new standard for aortic education.

2023

Gulf Aorta Summit — GAS2023

The Aortic Summit GAS2023 became a premier event within the Gulf Aorta framework — a platform equipping participants with cutting-edge knowledge and practical, personalised approaches to aortic disease. Interactive sessions led by expert faculty underscored a shared commitment to progress and innovation, and set the precedent for the Gulf Aorta Educational Foundation to annually recognise the leading contributors and co-believers shaping the event.

Its success was built on genuine partnerships with renowned experts, whose collaboration helped integrate the latest scientific advances into the summit's framework.

2024

Gulf Aorta Summit — GAS2024

GAS2024 extended that collaboration further, organised as a global expert hub with a curriculum spanning the latest advances in aortic and vascular interventional management. Globally renowned faculty and highly engaged interactive scientific sessions carried the summit to its strongest edition yet, deepening the international scientific contribution behind Gulf Aorta.

2026 →

Toward the ultimate vision

GAS has grown into a continental platform, uniting multidisciplinary specialists across the global aortic community around one goal: reducing the impact of aortic disease. Each edition brings cutting-edge research, interactive workshops and scientific sessions built to sharpen the global aortic workforce — and improve outcomes for patients.

Gulf Aorta Summit 2026, in Dubai, is the next chapter.

Where it's going

A continental platform, one summit at a time.

The Gulf Aorta Summit (GAS) has evolved into a continental platform, uniting multidisciplinary specialists from the global aortic community with the shared goal of reducing the impact of aortic diseases. The summit serves as a hub for innovation, education and collaboration, fostering advancements in aortic care.

This annual event brings cutting-edge research presentations, interactive workshops, scientific sessions and exclusive opportunities to meet with leading experts — collectively enhancing the skills of the global aortic workforce, and paving the way for improved patient outcomes.
